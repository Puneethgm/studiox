The Flow

From first message to paid trial, fully automated.

This is the actual path a lead takes through the system today — not a concept diagram. Every step below is live in production.

1

Lead arrives from any channel

A prospect fills out your branded lead-capture page, or messages your studio directly on WhatsApp or Facebook Messenger. Either way, they land in the same place: one lead record, tagged with source and campaign.

Branded lead form WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API) Facebook Messenger
2

Instant sync, zero data loss

The lead is written to your dashboard and queued to your Google Sheet in the same transaction. If Sheets is briefly unavailable, the sync retries automatically — nothing is ever dropped.

Postgres + outbox pattern Google Sheets auto-sync
3

Decision-tree engine checks first

Before any AI is involved, a rules engine handles structured moments — greetings, booking menus, FAQ shortcuts — so common paths reply instantly and consistently, every time.

Admin-configurable rules Runs pre-LLM
4

Studio knowledge base is consulted

Your pricing, class schedule, trainer bios, and FAQs are chunked, embedded, and stored as vectors. When a question needs studio-specific facts, the system retrieves and reranks the most relevant chunks before the AI answers.

Semantic chunking Vector search (pgvector) Reranking
5

A 4-tier AI waterfall writes the reply

The message is routed through the fastest capable model first, escalating only when needed — so replies stay both quick and reliable, and every attempt is logged for cost and latency.

Groq Llama 3.1 8B Groq Llama 3.3 70B Gemini 2.5 / 2.0 Flash Claude (final fallback)
6

Sentiment reads the room, pipeline updates itself

Each reply is scored for intent — positive, neutral, negative — and the lead's stage moves automatically: New → Contacted → Trial Booked → Member, or Dropped if it's a clear no. Your Kanban board updates live, no one has to drag a card.

Auto-advancing pipeline Live board (SSE)
7

"Yes" becomes a paid trial in one message

The moment a lead agrees to a trial, the system pulls the studio's real plan price, generates a Stripe checkout link, shortens it, and sends it straight into the same WhatsApp thread — no forms, no phone tag, no separate booking flow.

Stripe Checkout Dynamic plan pricing Shortened links Glofox first-session automation
